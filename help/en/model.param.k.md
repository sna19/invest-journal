# Years with a rise (K)

**What it is.** In how many of 10 years the share must have risen in this quarter.

**The idea.** The more years of rise required, the fewer coincidences, but also fewer candidates.

**How to use it.** More — stricter and fewer candidates, but one market crash can throw them all out. Less — more candidates, but more random ones too. Default 9.

**Reminder.** A strict choice protects from chance.

**Trap.** Loosening K until the list is long: those passing become random.

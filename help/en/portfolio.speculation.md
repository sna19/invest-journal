# Speculation

**What it is.** A limit on the share of positions of the kind «Speculation» and the loss allowed on them.

**The idea.** Rule 3. Curiosity is fine if its price is known in advance. A purchase «to see» without a limit easily loses a quarter of what went in.

**How to use it.** Mark speculation honestly. The play budget is refilled only by speculation's profit or once a year.

**Reminder.** It is a game. How much am I ready to lose?

**Trap.** Renaming a losing speculation «long-term».

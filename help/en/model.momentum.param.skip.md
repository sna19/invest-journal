# Skip the last month

**What it is.** The rise is counted to the end of the month before last — the last month is left out.

**The idea.** Over a single month prices often bounce back; without the last month the signal is less confused by that noise. Most momentum studies do so.

**How to use it.** On by default. Turn it off to see the difference in «Result».

**Reminder.** Yesterday's jump is not a trend.

**Trap.** Deciding by one month.

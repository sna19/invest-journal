# Stop, target and risk

**What it is.** The stop, the target, the review date and the trade's risk in money: (entry − stop) × quantity.

**The idea.** Rule 1: no exit condition, no trade. The position size comes from the risk, not from a feeling. Three positions without a stop made most of the portfolio's loss.

**How to use it.** Put the stop where the thesis becomes wrong (beyond a level, a recent low), not at a round percentage. A guide for the risk per trade is 0.5–1% of the portfolio; quantity = risk / (entry − stop).

**Reminder.** How much do I lose if I am wrong?

**Trap.** Moving the stop away when the price comes near it.

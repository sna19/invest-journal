# Reason labels

**What it is.** A seasonality that has an explanation repeats more often than a random one.

**The idea.** Seasonality with an economic reason (a report, a dividend, the industry's season) repeats more reliably than a coincidence.

**How to use it.** «Report» — the company publishes a report in this quarter, and the rise may be tied to it. «Dividend» — the quarter has a dividend date. «Sector seasonal» — in this quarter the share's whole sector beats the market, not just the share. A label does not prove the reason, but makes it plausible.

**Reminder.** Does the season have a reason?

**Trap.** Taking the mark for proof: it is an explanation, not a check.

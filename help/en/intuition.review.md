# Honest review

**What it is.** Your decisions over a month, a quarter or all time: how many came true, the purchases' result against the market and the holding time — by basis (intuition, hypothesis, model, consensus, mixed) and by type; the refusals and what the security did after them; the pause from the first entry to the decision; the weekly pick.

**The idea.** Intuition must prove itself with the same numbers as the model and the sources. Without a review the lucky «gut» decisions are remembered and the unlucky ones forgotten. The source «Me» in the sources' rating is the hypotheses of the source «Own idea».

**How to use it.** Once a month open «Month», once a quarter — «Quarter». Compare the row «Intuition» with the rest: if it is worse over ten decisions and more — lean on the rules and the model. In «Refusals» see whether you more often avoid a loss or miss a rise.

**Reminder.** What to trust — the gut or the numbers?

**Trap.** Drawing conclusions from two or three decisions.

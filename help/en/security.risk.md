# Risk: how hard it will swing

**What it is.** How much the share moves with the market and how many players bet on its fall.

**The idea.** Risk is not a reason not to buy but a measure: how much to buy and where to put the stop. A share that swings twice as hard as the market is held in a smaller piece.

**How to use it.**

- **Beta** — how much the share moves with the market (S&P 500) on average. 1 — like the market; 1.5 — 15% when the market moves 10%; under 0.8 — calmer (utilities, consumer staples); negative — against the market (rare, e.g. gold). **3-year beta** — the same over a longer stretch: if it differs a lot from the usual one, the share's character changed recently.
- **Short, % of float** — the share of the float sold in expectation of a fall. Up to 3–5% — usual; over 10% — many players expect trouble (worth knowing what); over 20% — the share can jump when the short sellers rush to close, but that is a game, not a thesis.
- A high beta — a smaller share of the portfolio (the «Risk» screen shows its part of the portfolio's swings) and a stop further away: «Protection» computes it from the share's usual move (ATR).

**When it matters.** When choosing the size of a position and the stop; when the short interest of a position rises — check the news and the thesis.

**Reminder.** The harder it swings, the smaller the piece.

**Trap.** Beta is counted from the past and measures only the link to the market: a low-beta share can have crashes of its own (a report, a lawsuit) that beta does not see.

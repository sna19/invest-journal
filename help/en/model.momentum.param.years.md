# Years of history

**What it is.** The length of the window the table, the curves and the checks are computed on. The window's last 3 years are the check years.

**The idea.** A longer window has seen more different markets. Some funds have a shorter history — before their start they simply take no part in the choice.

**How to use it.** 10 years by default. Longer — more crises in the sample.

**Reminder.** One decade is still one story.

**Trap.** Shortening the window to drop an awkward year.

# Own ideas and outside sources

**What it is.** A comparison of hypotheses from the «Own idea» source with all outside sources together — by the same rules as a source's rating: closed on time, the types «Recommendation», «Target price» and «Opinion»; «News» — apart.

**The idea.** Your own ideas deserve the same strict check as others': they are often worse than they seem.

**How to use it.**

**Why.** To see whether reading articles gives more than your own thoughts — and the other way round.

**How to read.** The share that came true with its interval and the mean excess over the benchmark. If the intervals overlap a lot, there is no difference to see yet.

**Reminder.** Are my ideas better than others'?

**Trap.** Recording your own ideas less often than others': the comparison becomes unfair.

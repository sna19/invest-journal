# Job risk

**What it is.** The employer's sector next to that sector's share in the portfolio.

**The idea.** The salary is an asset too, and it is in the employer's industry. The portfolio should balance the job's risk, not double it: a bad year for that industry hits both the income and the savings.

**How to use it.** Keep the sector's share under the limit. Over it, new money goes to other sectors.

**Reminder.** A bad year for the job's industry hits twice.

**Trap.** Taking the knowledge of the industry from work for an edge and loading the same sector.

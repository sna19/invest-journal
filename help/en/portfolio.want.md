# «Want to buy» — how to work with it

**What it is.** A planned purchase: the security, the wanted price, the amount and the trade conditions — the thesis, «what proves me wrong», the stop, the target, the review date. The form fills them itself: the price from the last close, the stop, the target and the amount from the protective plan (by ATR and the risk budget), the thesis from the quant score or the idea of the week. Change what you disagree with.

**The idea.** A purchase is decided calmly, in advance, and carried out by the plan — no fuss at the screen. Buying «at market» in a moment of excitement is expensive; waiting for your price is discipline.

**How to use it.**

1. **Added — where to look.** «More → Signals», the block «Want to buy»: every planned purchase, the price now and the wanted one, how far to go, the stop, the target, how many days it waits and what to do. Also the section «Want to buy» in the portfolio and the security card.
2. **You wait.** Nothing to do: when the price reaches the wanted one, a signal comes (on the main screen, in «Signals», and as a push if «Want to buy» is on in «Settings → Notifications»). You can place a limit order at the wanted price at the broker right away — then the purchase happens by itself.
3. **The price came.** Re-read the thesis. Still alive — «Bought» on the security card: the stop and the target carry over from the plan, and after the purchase the cheat sheet shows what to place at the broker.
4. **The price fell under the plan's stop before the purchase.** By your own plan the idea is wrong: «Changed my mind» — or «New plan» with a new level and a new reason.
5. **It waits over 30 days, or its review date came.** Decide: buy now, drop it, or a new plan with a new date.

**Reminder.** «Want to buy» is a plan with a price and a stop, not a bookmark.

**Trap.** A forever «want to buy» list that is never carried out nor reviewed — or buying above the wanted price «not to miss it».

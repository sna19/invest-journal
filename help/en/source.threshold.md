# Why «few data»

While a source has fewer than **10 closed hypotheses**, no percent is shown — only a count, for example «7 of 9».

**Why.** On a few cases the share that came true jumps with luck: 3 out of 3 happens to any source. The interval at such numbers is too wide to rely on (3 out of 3 — from 44% to 100%), and a single «100%» would look like a conclusion.

**Next.** From 10 to 29 closed, the percent is shown with an interval and the «preliminary» mark. From 30 — without the mark.

**How long to wait.** At 2–3 hypotheses a month per source the first 10 closed gather in a few months: the default horizon is 3 months. For the rating to appear sooner and be honest, record all checkable claims of the source, not only the ones you liked.

# Score

**What it is.** The score shows how confidently the share beat the market in this quarter: the mean excess divided by its spread over the years.

**The idea.** The score weighs the mean rise, its spread and the number of years: steadiness matters more than one bright year.

**How to use it.** An even result gives a high score, an uneven one a low score, even if the mean is large. A score above 2 is notable, below 1 is weak. With few years the score is lowered on purpose.

**Reminder.** Is it steady, or one lucky year?

**Trap.** Taking a security with a high mean but a low score.

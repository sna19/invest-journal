# Countries

**What it is.** The list of funds the model chooses from — country funds traded in the US. SPY is the US market itself, a participant too.

**The idea.** A wider choice — more chance to find a strong country, but also more small markets with sharp jumps.

**How to use it.** Add a fund's ticker or remove one with the cross; the change is saved at once. A new fund's monthly prices load by themselves within a minute.

**Reminder.** The list sets the game — change it rarely.

**Trap.** Adding a country because it has already risen.

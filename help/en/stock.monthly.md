# Mosaic by month and year

**What it is.** A table: rows are years, columns are months, a cell is the month's change, its colour — rise or fall; on the right — the year's total. Switches: months or quarters, «as is» or «vs market». The months you held the share are outlined. A portfolio has the same mosaic.

**The idea.** This is how funds show their history: not only the total but when it was made — evenly every month or in one burst, and where the standstill began.

**How to use it.** Look at the stripes: a row of pale cells after bright ones — a standstill; a row of light red and one bright green — a slow fall and a spike. «Vs market» removes the market's common move. A tap on a cell — its numbers and that month on the chart.

**Reminder.** Was the profit made evenly or in one jump?

**Trap.** Looking for seasonality in 2–3 years of the mosaic: that is noise. For seasonality there is the quarterly model with its placebo check.

# Compare with the market or the sector

Variant B only. **The market** — the share's quarterly rise minus the rise of the S&P 500 (US) or TA-125 (Israel). **The sector** — minus the average rise of the shares of its sector in the universe: this removes the seasonality of the whole industry and leaves what sets the share apart from its neighbours. If the sector has fewer than 5 shares, the market is used. Default: the market.

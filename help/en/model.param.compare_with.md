# Compare with the market or the sector

**What it is.** Variant B only. **The market** — the share's quarterly rise minus the rise of the S&P 500 (US) or TA-125 (Israel).

**The idea.** A rise together with the whole market or sector is not the security's merit. The comparison separates its own strength.

**How to use it.** **The sector** — minus the average rise of the shares of its sector in the universe: this removes the seasonality of the whole industry and leaves what sets the share apart from its neighbours. If the sector has fewer than 5 shares, the market is used. Default: the market.

**Reminder.** Is the security strong, or did everything rise?

**Trap.** Comparing with a sector of few securities: its mean is random.

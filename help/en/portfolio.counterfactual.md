# What if into the index

**What it is.** The result of the same cash flows put into the index (SPY or TA-125), and the difference with your portfolio.

**The idea.** Any active strategy has to prove it beats the index. Until it does, the index is the default.

**How to use it.** Look once a quarter. Lagging several quarters in a row is a reason to cut the active part in favour of the core.

**Reminder.** Am I better than just the index?

**Trap.** Comparing with the index over a conveniently chosen period.

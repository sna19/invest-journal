# Simulation and real money

The same figures separately for hypotheses in the «simulation» mode and in the «real money» mode.

**Why.** To check whether you pick worse (or better) ideas for your money than the ones you just record. If real ones are steadily lower, the choice of ideas for money is worth revisiting.

**What counts.** Hypotheses closed on time of the types «Recommendation», «Target price» and «Opinion»; «News» — apart.

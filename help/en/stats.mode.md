# Simulation and real money

**What it is.** The same figures separately for hypotheses in the «simulation» mode and in the «real money» mode.

**The idea.** If the real-money ideas are worse than the simulated ones, the problem is not the ideas but how you choose what to act on.

**How to use it.**

**Why.** To check whether you pick worse (or better) ideas for your money than the ones you just record. If real ones are steadily lower, the choice of ideas for money is worth revisiting.

**What counts.** Hypotheses closed on time of the types «Recommendation», «Target price» and «Opinion»; «News» — apart.

**Reminder.** Do I put money on my best ideas or my worst?

**Trap.** Comparing the modes when one of them has a handful of hypotheses.

# Daily turnover, at least

**What it is.** The share's average daily turnover over the last 12 months in dollars.

**The idea.** Without turnover you can neither enter nor exit at a fair price; the paper result will not repeat in a trade.

**How to use it.** Higher — only liquid shares that can be bought and sold without losing on the spread. Lower — more small companies, where both seasonality and data errors are more common. Default $1M.

**Reminder.** Can I get out when I want?

**Trap.** Taking illiquid securities: the spread eats the seasonal rise.

# About the company

**What it is.** What the company does or what the fund invests in: the data provider's (Yahoo) summary, the city and the country, the staff, the site.

**The idea.** Buy what you understand. If you cannot say in two sentences how the company earns its money, the thesis rests on the price, not on the business.

**How to use it.** Before «Bought» read the summary and check: is the thesis about what the company really does? For a fund look at what it invests in — it is not always what its name promises.

**Reminder.** How does this company make its money?

**Trap.** Buying by the ticker and the chart without knowing what business stands behind them.

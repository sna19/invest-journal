# Review at the sale

**What it is.** The reason for the exit, whether the rule was kept, and the matrix «good / bad process × result + / −».

**The idea.** A good decision can lose money, a bad one can make it. You can learn only from the process: one lucky trade does not prove skill.

**How to use it.** Review every sale, profitable ones above all. The «bad process + profit» cell is the most dangerous one: it teaches the wrong thing.

**Reminder.** Judge the decision, not the result.

**Trap.** Skipping the review when the trade made money.

# Costs and holding

**What it is.** Every change of shares costs a fee, and every sale at a profit — 25% tax.

**The idea.** Trades and taxes eat the return unnoticed: a strategy strong before costs can be worse than the index after them.

**How to use it.** Changing the whole portfolio once a quarter eats noticeably into the return. So the return is shown twice: before the costs and after. The second one matters for the decision. «Hold if chosen again» means that a share on the next quarter's list is neither sold nor bought again. Set the fee your broker charges.

**Reminder.** What is left after the fee and the tax?

**Trap.** Judging the model by its return before costs.

# Discipline

**What it is.** The share of trades made by the rules and their result against trades outside the rules; the result by kind of trade.

**The idea.** Rules must prove their worth in numbers — on your own trades, not in books.

**How to use it.** Once a quarter. Trades outside the rules do worse — an argument to yourself to keep the rules. They do better over a large sample — reconsider the rule.

**Reminder.** Rules are checked with numbers.

**Trap.** Drawing conclusions from 3–5 trades.

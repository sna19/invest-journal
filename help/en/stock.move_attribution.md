# Move split

**What it is.** The day move of the market and of the sector is taken out of the security's day move: what is left is what happened to the company itself.

**The idea.** When the whole sector falls, the company's news explains nothing — and the other way round. A fall together with the sector is the sector's risk (and your job's); a fall deeper than the sector is a signal to check the thesis on the company.

**How to use it.** Look at the largest part. The market or the sector — a decision at the portfolio level (the sector's share, a hedge). The share itself — the company's news and a check of the thesis.

**Reminder.** Whose move is it — the market's, the sector's or the company's?

**Trap.** Looking in the company's news for the cause of a fall the market made entirely.

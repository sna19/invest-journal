# Thesis

**What it is.** One or two sentences: why this trade, and what will prove you wrong.

**The idea.** Without a written thesis, a month later you cannot tell «the thesis is broken» from «it is just scary». The review of losing positions in November 2026 ran into exactly that: the reason for the purchase was not kept.

**How to use it.** The thesis and what disproves it come before the «Bought» button. At the review, check the thesis, not the price.

**Reminder.** If you cannot put it into words, do not buy.

**Trap.** «It will go up» is not a thesis. A thesis answers «why, and by when».

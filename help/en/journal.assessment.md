# Observation's assessment

**What it is.** An observation in a hypothesis's diary is marked: the thesis is strengthening, weakening or unchanged. The hypothesis itself does not change — its key fields are locked.

**The idea.** A hypothesis is checked at its term, but on the way you see where the thesis goes. A chain «strengthening → weakening → weakening» is seen in advance, not in hindsight.

**How to use it.** In a hypothesis's card — «The hypothesis's diary» → «Write»: an observation opens with three assessment buttons. Any entry can become a hypothesis — «→ Hypothesis»: the source «Own idea», the entry's text becomes the thesis, the entry and the hypothesis are linked.

**Reminder.** Has the thesis grown stronger or weaker?

**Trap.** Marking «strengthening» every time the price rises.

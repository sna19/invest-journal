# Path marks

**What it is.** Automatic marks on the card, in the list of positions and on the candidates. The defaults (changed in the settings): «fading» — at least 10% a year over 3 years, but at most 2% over 12 months or 5 pp under the market; «stuck» — 250 trading days without a new high and the price within ±10% over 12 months; «spike» — at least half of 3 years' profit made in 1–2 months; «bounce» — −15% or worse over 12 months and +15% or better over the last month.

**The idea.** Nobody opens every chart every day. A mark says by itself that the character of the movement changed and sends a signal to the main screen.

**How to use it.** A mark is not an order to sell but a reason to review the thesis: open the mosaic and the ladder, write down a conclusion. The thesis is intact — press «Reviewed», and the mark will not return until the picture changes again.

**Reminder.** A mark is a question, not an answer.

**Trap.** Tweaking the thresholds until an awkward mark goes away.

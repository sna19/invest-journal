# Account and users

**What it is.** The login, each user's own data, the language, the password and signing out everywhere.

**The idea.** The journal is personal: each person has their own decisions and statistics, or someone else's trades mix with your conclusions.

**How to use it.**

**Sign-in.** Several people use the journal, each with their own login and password. A sign-in is remembered on the device for a year. While you keep using the journal the term renews by itself, so you will hardly ever sign in again. The lock in front of the app (the shared site password) stays as it was.

**Everyone has their own.** Portfolios, trades, hypotheses, sources and stats belong to each user, and others do not see them. Only market data are shared: prices, quotes, the dollar rate, the ticker base. That is why the data update settings are shared too.

**Language.** The RU / EN switch changes the interface language. The choice is kept for the user on all their devices.

**Change password.** You need the current password and the new one, twice. After the change other devices are signed out, while this device stays signed in.

**Sign out on all devices.** Useful if a phone is lost. All sign-ins of this user stop working.

**Users** (only the administrator, the first user, sees this):
- «Add a user» creates a new account. At the first sign-in the person gets an empty journal.
- «New password» next to a user sets a password for them if they forgot theirs. Their devices are signed out.

**Reminder.** My journal — my decisions.

**Trap.** Sharing your login with someone else: their trades spoil your statistics.

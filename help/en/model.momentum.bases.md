# Bases to compare with

**What it is.** Three ways without the model: SPY — buy the US market and hold; «All countries equally» — every fund of the list in equal parts, rebalanced each January; «Last year's leaders» — each January the 4 best countries of the past calendar year, held for a year.

**The idea.** The model makes sense only if it beats the simple. The third base is how countries are usually bought by hand: last year's leader, with no exit rule.

**How to use it.** Look at «Yearly», «Worst fall» and Sharpe (return above the bills per unit of swings). The switch on top — the whole window or the check years only (the last 3). A commission and a tax on gains when selling are taken from all but SPY: it is never sold.

**Reminder.** Not better than SPY over 10 years — why the complexity?

**Trap.** Comparing the return only: a model with a deep fall is hard to sit through live.

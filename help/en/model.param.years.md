# Years of history

**What it is.** Over how many recent years the quarter is looked at. More years — a more reliable choice, but fewer shares with such a long history and more weight of old years.

**The idea.** More years — more different markets in the sample and less chance, but fewer securities with a full history.

**How to use it.** Fewer — more shares, but also more chance coincidences. Default 10.

**Reminder.** How many years does the conclusion stand on?

**Trap.** Shortening the history to drop an awkward year.

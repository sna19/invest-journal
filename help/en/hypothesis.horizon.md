# Horizon

The date on which the source's claim is checked.

**Take the horizon from the article.** «Over 12 months», «by the end of the year», «in the coming quarter» — choose the nearest option or an exact date.

**The article names no horizon** — leave «Not named»: 3 months from the record date are set, and the hypothesis is marked «default». Such hypotheses can be filtered in the list.

**What happens on that date.** The hypothesis closes by itself: the close of the last trading day not later than the horizon is taken, the return of the security and of the benchmark (S&P 500 for the US, TA-125 for TASE) is computed, and the result — it came true if the security beat the benchmark.

The horizon does not change after saving.

# Signals

**What it is.** One «what to do» screen: the app checks your positions every day and once a week chooses ideas to buy.

- **Sell** — the hard rules, decided in advance: a stop or trailing level is broken (by the close or the quote), a model's period is over. Sold — enter the sale in the position's card.
- **Review** — reasons to open the thesis, not orders: the target reached (take it or switch to a trailing stop), the review date came, a path mark («fading», «stuck»…), the quant score turned red, the price went under its 200-day average within the last 10 trading days (a crossing, not a long state), the position is over its set part of the portfolio (15%) and over two equal parts (25% with 8 positions), a loss of 20% or more from the purchase price (the card shows «Drawdown review»). «Keep — because…» writes the reason in the diary, and the signal stays quiet for 30 days.
- **Place at the broker** — positions without a stop, or with a stop-limit but no target where one is advised: a ready plan and a cheat sheet with «I placed it».
- **Buy — the week's ideas** — up to 5 ideas (by default on Sunday at 20:00 Israel time, US and TASE). The funnel: liquid shares (turnover from $1M a day, price from $5), not held → a green quant score (so the price is above its SMA200) → no «fading» or «stuck» mark → no report in the next 7 days → the target at least 1.5 risks away (otherwise a resistance is near and the gain small) → within the limits of the job's industry and speculation → not «duplicating» what is held. The funnel's green shares join the daily price loading by themselves: without 200 daily closes the trend is unknown and the share cannot pass — that is what kept the Israeli shares out, they had no daily history. The order — by the quant score, with a bonus if the share is among the quarterly model's candidates. Each idea has its why, a stop or trailing stop, a target, a size (1% of the portfolio at risk) and the loss to the stop. All values are set at the bottom of the screen and in «Settings → Protective plan».

**The idea.** Decisions by rules, not by mood, and every rule is checked. Each idea is recorded as a hypothesis of the source «Signals» for 3 months and bought by the source's virtual portfolio; skipped ideas are measured too. «Sources» will show whether the system beats the market — and after 1–2 months of data a breakdown will show which filter helps and which does not.

**How to use it.** Once a day — «Sell» and «Review», once a week (a notification comes) — the ideas. The block «Want to buy» — every planned purchase and what to do with each now (more in its «?»). For an idea: read the why, open the card (mosaic, ratios, news), decide: «Bought» (the form with the plan and the link to the idea), «Want to buy», or «Skip» with a reason. The first 3 months the signals are kept in simulation; real money — when the source's statistics justify it (from 30 closed ideas, better than the market and a random choice).

**Reminder.** A signal is a proposal with a plan; the decision is yours.

**Trap.** Taking every idea without reading the card, or ignoring «Sell» because «it will bounce now».

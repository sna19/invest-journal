# Rise over L months

**What it is.** A country's signal is its fund's rise over L months, by month-end prices with dividends. The classic is 12.

**The idea.** A short window catches a change of leader faster, but errs more often and trades more; a long one is calmer but late.

**How to use it.** Change L — the table is recomputed at once, without saving. Save only when you decided to trade the new way.

**Reminder.** Change the rule — change the experiment.

**Trap.** Taking the L that makes the past look best.

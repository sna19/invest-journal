# Monthly split

**What it is.** The candidate's average result in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd month of the quarter over 10 years.

**The idea.** A quarter's average rise does not say when it comes. If the rise usually comes in the 1st month and this year it did not, the seasonal driver did not work. If usually in the 3rd, lagging at the start of the quarter is normal.

**How to use it.** Before any decision inside the quarter, look at which month the rise usually falls on and compare with what is happening this year.

**Reminder.** A quarter's rise ≠ a rise every month. Look at when it usually comes.

**Trap.** Thinking the rise is «postponed» and has to come.

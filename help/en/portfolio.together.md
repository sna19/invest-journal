# All together

The sum of all real portfolios in one currency (shekels by default) at today's Bank of Israel rate — labelled «at the rate of the date». The total currency is switched at the top.

For a dollar portfolio there is one more line: what its dollars are worth in shekels now and what they would be worth at the rate of your own conversions. The difference is what the dollar rate gave (or took).

The screen only adds up: nothing changes in the portfolios themselves, their return stays in their currency.

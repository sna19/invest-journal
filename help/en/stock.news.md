# News

**What it is.** All the news about the security, its sector and the market in one feed, with links and times, and a short summary of «why it moves».

**The idea.** News helps you see whether your thesis is broken. Predicting the price from it is almost impossible: by the time you read the headline, the price has usually taken it in. After any move it is easy to find a nice explanation in hindsight.

**How to use it.** A strong move → first the split (market / sector / the share itself) → then the news of the level where the move is largest → the question: does this change my thesis? No — the stop works, you do nothing. Yes — you decide on the position without waiting for the stop. An idea from the news goes into the journal as a hypothesis, not straight into a trade.

**Reminder.** News explains the past. One question: is my thesis broken?

**Trap.** Buying or selling on the emotion of a headline on a day of a strong move.

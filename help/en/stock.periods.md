# Return over periods

**What it is.** The price rise with dividends over 1 and 3 months, half a year, the year to date, 1, 3 and 5 years, and for a position also «since the purchase». Under each number — the same over the market (the S&P 500 or the TA-125). Periods longer than a year are shown both as a total and «a year».

**The idea.** One number over 3 years hides the path. +85% over 3 years is 22.8% a year; but if the last year was 0%, all the profit was made earlier, and the share is standing still now.

**How to use it.** Read the row from the long periods to the short. The short ones weaker than the long (as a year rate) — the growth slows; stronger — it speeds up. Compare with the market: +10% with the market at +15% is lagging.

**Reminder.** What matters is not what it made but what it makes now.

**Trap.** Comparing a 3-year total with another share's yearly return, or being glad of a rise the market gave everyone.

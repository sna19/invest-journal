# Stats

**What it is.** A summary over all closed hypotheses: how honest your confidence is, how your own ideas differ from outside sources, simulation from real money, and how tag topics work.

**The idea.** Statistics are the process' mirror: they show where decisions work and where you fool yourself.

**How to use it.**

**What counts.** Only hypotheses closed at their horizon. Closed early and cancelled ones are not in the rating; «News» goes apart in the comparisons — as in the sources table.

**The same thresholds.** Fewer than 10 closed — «few data» and only a count; 10–29 — a percent with an interval and the «preliminary» mark; from 30 — a percent with an interval.

**The «Without the default term» filter** removes hypotheses where the article named no horizon and 3 months were set.

**The order of blocks** can change: hold a block or drag by ≡; the order is remembered on this device.

**Reminder.** What does my journal say about me?

**Trap.** Looking at the statistics only when things go well.

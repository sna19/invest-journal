# History

**What it is.** The path of a security or a hypothesis on one timeline with the price: the hypothesis → observations → the decision to buy → observations → the decision to sell → the conclusion. Each step is a mark on the chart and a line under it.

**The idea.** You see what you thought and did at which price: where you waited, where you hurried, where the decision followed the move.

**How to use it.** Open «History» in a security's or a hypothesis's card. A tap on a mark highlights its line. B — buy, A — add, T — trim, S — sell, H — hold, Hy — hypothesis, C — conclusion.

**Reminder.** Did the decision come before the move or after it?

**Trap.** Looking at the history of the lucky securities only.

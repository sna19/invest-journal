# Risk map

**What it is.** Circles of the positions: the size is the share of money, related securities stand close. Three numbers on top: how many independent bets, how much the portfolio swings in a year and how tied it is to your job.

**The idea.** Markowitz's theory: a portfolio's risk is not the sum of its securities' risks, it depends on how much they move together. 13 securities that fall at the same time are not 13 bets but far fewer. In crises the ties grow stronger, and diversification weakens exactly when it is needed.

**How to use it.** «+ a share on the map» puts any security among your positions as a dashed circle — just to see what it moves with (a tap on the circle — its most tied positions); several in a row, «×» removes; not saved, gone when you leave the screen. Look for clusters of circles — that is one bet, however many tickers it has. A position whose share of risk is clearly larger than its share of money is the first to cut. A cluster next to the «job» circle is a risk that hits twice.

**Reminder.** How many independent bets do I really have?

**Trap.** Thinking past correlations last forever: they change, and in a panic almost everything falls together.

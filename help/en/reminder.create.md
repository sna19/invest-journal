# Reminders

**What it is.** A reminder to an entry or a decision: on a date or on a condition — the price above or below a level, a change over N days, a position's drawdown from the average price, N days before a report, the start of a quarter, the end of a hypothesis's term. It is checked with the day's prices and with the quotes in trading hours.

**The idea.** «I will come back to it later» without a reminder turns into «I forgot». A condition on the price or a report brings you to the entry exactly when it is needed.

**How to use it.** In an entry — «Remind», choose the condition. A fired one stands in «Signals» on the main screen. «Defer» on a security's card sets its reminder itself. Each reminder's history is in its entry, «history».

**Reminder.** When do I want to come back to this — and on what sign?

**Trap.** Setting a reminder instead of a decision.

# Entries upwards along the links

**What it is.** An entry about a security is also seen above it: in its sector, in the funds and indices it is compared with, in the portfolios it is or was in, in the model runs where it was a candidate. Such entries are marked «via AAPL». Entries do not go down: an entry about a sector does not appear in each of its securities.

**The idea.** A thought about a security is also a thought about its sector and the portfolio. So a portfolio shows everything you thought about its securities without writing it twice.

**How to use it.** In a sector and at a fund or an index the switch «including the securities' entries» under «more filters» leaves only the entries about the object itself. A source also shows the entries about its hypotheses — «via №12».

**Reminder.** Write where you think — the entry will be found above too.

**Trap.** Writing the same in the security and in the portfolio: the feed doubles.

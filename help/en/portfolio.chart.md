# Portfolio return chart

**What it is.** The portfolio's return (TWR) day by day since the first trade — next to the benchmarks over the same period and, if you choose, other portfolios.

**The idea.** A return makes sense only next to the benchmark: +10% with the market at +20% is lagging.

**How to use it.**

**Lines.** The portfolio — solid; S&P 500 in shekels and TA-125 — dashed (price indexes, without dividends); S&P 500 in dollars — by a button. Buttons with portfolio names add their lines: that is how a real portfolio is compared with virtual ones and with the sources' automatic portfolios.

A long history is shown by weeks; days of deposits and withdrawals are not skipped, so the last point matches the return in the summary.

**Reminder.** Am I better than the market, or just with it?

**Trap.** Being glad of the portfolio's rise without looking at the index line.

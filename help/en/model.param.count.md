# Candidates

**What it is.** How many shares the model picks for the quarter and records as hypotheses.

**The idea.** Several candidates spread the risk of one security; too many dilute the choice to the market average.

**How to use it.** More — a wider portfolio and less weight of one share, but weaker ones get in too. Default 10.

**Reminder.** How many securities will I really follow?

**Trap.** Setting many candidates so that «something surely works».

# The year

**What it is.** The realized profit and loss of the real portfolios over a calendar year — from the sales, by FIFO, in the portfolio's currency; dividends next to it. An estimate, not a tax calculation.

**The idea.** By the end of the year you see what is already locked in — and can calmly decide whether a sale is needed before 31 December, not on the last day.

**How to use it.** In December a reminder appears in «Signals». Check the numbers against the broker's report; the tax is calculated by the broker or an adviser.

**Reminder.** What have I already locked in this year?

**Trap.** Selling a good position only for the tax.

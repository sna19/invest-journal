# Signal type

**What it is.** What exactly the source claims. The type decides how the hypothesis enters the source's rating.

**The idea.** A source is judged for what it claimed itself; your reading of a news item is your hypothesis, not theirs.

**How to use it.**

- **Recommendation** — «buy» or «sell» is said outright. The direction comes from the article.
- **Target price** — a price is named, usually with a horizon («$250 in 12 months»). Record the price and the horizon from the article.
- **Opinion** — a column with a clear stance on the security, without direct advice.
- **News** — a fact without a view («the company won a contract»). The direction is your reading, so it is always marked as «mine».

**Why «News» counts apart.** A source's rating is built on the first three types — there the source itself set the direction. News checks your reading more than the outlet, so it is shown as a separate row and is not in the rating.

Record all checkable claims of an article, not only the ones you liked — otherwise the source's stats come out selective.

**Reminder.** What exactly does the source claim?

**Trap.** Recording a news item as a recommendation: the source is scored for your conclusion.

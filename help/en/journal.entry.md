# Entry

**What it is.** One diary entry: its kind (note, observation, decision, conclusion), the text, what it is linked to and your tags. Opened from a security's, a portfolio's or a hypothesis's screen, it is linked to it at once — a link can be removed or added.

**The idea.** One thing for everything you write: a note to a trade, an observation on a hypothesis, a thought about a candidate, a day's record. Then everything is in one place and seen from every screen it belongs to.

**How to use it.** Write briefly: what you see, what you think, what you will do. **Bold** and lists with «- » work. The draft is kept on the device until you tap «Save». «Reply» continues the entry as a chain; «Edit» saves a new version, the earlier text stays in «edits».

**Reminder.** What do I think now — and why?

**Trap.** Rewriting an old entry as if you had known everything in advance: edits are visible, a new entry is more honest.

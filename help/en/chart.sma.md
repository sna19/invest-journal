# Moving averages on the chart

**What it is.** SMA lines of 20, 50 and 200 days: the short, medium and long trend.

**The idea.** It is a smoothed price with a lag. It shows the direction, not the future. Crossings in a sideways market are often false.

**How to use it.** Price above SMA200 and SMA50 above SMA200 — an uptrend. Price at SMA200 is a key level: the stop goes beyond it, not on it.

**Abbreviations.** SMA — Simple Moving Average; EMA — Exponential Moving Average.

**Reminder.** The lines describe the past.

**Trap.** Buying on a «golden cross» without an exit plan.

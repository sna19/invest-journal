# Drawdown from the high

**What it is.** The «under water» chart: how many percent the price is under its previous high and how many days passed without a new one. Below — the three deepest drawdowns: depth, length, time to recover.

**The idea.** Professionals judge risk not only by the depth of a fall but by time: a share that has not made a new high for two years is stuck, even if it did not fall.

**How to use it.** Look at the days without a new high and compare the current drawdown with the past ones. Deeper or longer than usual — open the thesis and check whether it broke.

**Reminder.** How long am I ready to wait for a new high?

**Trap.** Thinking: it always recovered, so it will recover now.

# Disposition effect

**What it is.** The profit taken against the loss left open, and the holding times of winners and losers.

**The idea.** People sell what rises too early and hold what falls too long. The result is a portfolio made of losses.

**How to use it.** If losing positions are held longer than winning ones — check the stops and do the «Position review».

**Reminder.** Cut the losses, let the profits run.

**Trap.** Selling the best to «lock it in» and keeping the worst.

# Not buying and Defer

**What it is.** A decision without a trade. «Not buying» — a conscious refusal with a reason and a date to check it. «Defer» — come back to the security on a set day.

**The idea.** A refusal is a decision too, and it can be checked: did you avoid a loss or miss a rise. Unwritten refusals are forgotten, and it seems you always miss the best.

**How to use it.** In a security's card — «Not buying» or «Defer», the basis and a sentence or two of «why». The reminder on the set day comes with the «Signals» block.

**Reminder.** Why not now?

**Trap.** Deferring again and again instead of deciding.

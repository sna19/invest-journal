# Market cap

Which companies by size go into the universe. By default — all.

**In dollars** — groups by market capitalisation: mega (over $200B), large ($10–200B), mid ($2–10B), small ($0.3–2B), micro (under $0.3B). The bounds can be changed («bounds, $B»). Large companies move more calmly and have fewer data errors, but also less seasonality. Small and micro give more candidates and more noise: one report or a data error changes a year's number. Next to each group — how many shares of the universe it holds.

**By market** — the top 20%, the middle and the bottom 30% by market cap, separately in the US and on TASE. Otherwise almost all Israeli companies end up «small».

The filter changes the universe, so the «by chance» estimate changes too. The market cap comes from the Yahoo profile and is refreshed weekly. In the check without peeking the past market cap is approximate (today's number of shares × the price then), and that peeks: companies that grew count as large in hindsight.

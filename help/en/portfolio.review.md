# Position review

**What it is.** For each open position — the thesis, the answer to «would I buy it today at this price?» and the exit condition.

**The idea.** Holding a position means buying it again every day. The market does not care what you paid: the purchase price is a sunk cost.

**How to use it.** Once a quarter go through all positions. A «no» → sell the same day or write a new thesis with an exit condition.

**Reminder.** Would I buy it today at this price?

**Trap.** «I will wait until it is back to zero».

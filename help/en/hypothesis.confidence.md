# Confidence

**What it is.** Optional. How much you yourself believe the source's claim, in percent — from 1 to 99.

**The idea.** A confidence written in advance is the only way to learn whether your feelings are honest: memory rewrites them after the result.

**How to use it.**

**Why put it in advance.** After a few dozen hypotheses you will see whether your confidence is honest: if only half comes true where you put 70%, you overrate your feelings. That is the «Stats» screen, calibration.

**When to skip it.** The main thing in the journal is checking the source, not your opinion. No feeling — leave it empty: calibration counts only hypotheses with a percent.

Confidence does not change after saving.

**Reminder.** What percent do I give — and does that many come true?

**Trap.** Giving 80% out of habit: the calibration will show half come true, and decisions are made on false confidence.

# Against the usual quarter

**What it is.** How much better the chosen quarter is than the same share's usual quarter, and in how many of 10 years it was above the usual.

**The idea.** A share with a strong trend is up in almost any quarter, and «10 of 10» speaks of the trend, not of the season. There is seasonality only if the quarter is systematically better than the same share's other quarters. Example: EMBJ grew about 9 times since 2022 — its Q4 «10 of 10» has to be checked exactly this way.

**How to use it.** Look at the second line under the table of years. An excess around zero or «above the usual» in fewer than 6 of 10 years — most likely no seasonality: you are buying the trend, and set the stop by the trend.

**Reminder.** Is this quarter better than this same share's usual quarter?

**Trap.** Taking a share's 10-year rise for a seasonal pattern.

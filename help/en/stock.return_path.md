# Where the profit was made

**What it is.** The chosen period's total laid out by years or quarters as a ladder. Example: +85% = +48 pp in year 1 + 37 pp in year 2 + 0 in year 3. Each step shows its part of the total and the year's own rise (+48%, +25%, 0%).

**The idea.** This is the direct answer to «when was it earned». Yearly rises multiply, they do not add up, so a step's part counts from what was already made, and the steps add up exactly to the total.

**How to use it.** The last step small or negative on a tall ladder — the profit is in the past. Check it against the thesis: is it about the past or the future?

**Reminder.** Was this profit made while I held it, or before?

**Trap.** Holding a share «for its merits» of past years.

# Screener

**What it is.** A search of both markets' shares by any of their sides at once: size, valuation (P/E, P/S), dividends, revenue growth, quality (ROE, net margin, debt), risk (beta), the return over 1 year and 3 months, liquidity, the quant score's colour and the trend (above its SMA200). The result is a table sorted by a tap on any header.

**The idea.** Not scrolling through thousands of tickers but narrowing at once to the ones that fit your thought: «cheap and sound», «dividend», «strong in a trend». A screener starts the study, it does not decide: next come the card, «Compare with», the quant score, the plan and the stop.

**How to use it.**

1. **Ready screens** on top: «Green in a trend» (green quant score, above SMA200, liquid), «Dividend» (DY 3%+, P/E up to 25), «Cheap and sound» (P/E up to 15, ROE 15%+, debt up to 100% of equity), «Fast growth» (revenue +20%+, up over the year), «Calm and big» (beta up to 0.8, market cap 10B+). A tap fills the filters; they can be adjusted.
2. **Your own filters**: market, sector and industry, size, the quant score's colour, «above SMA200», «liquid» ($1M+ a day), and «min / max» for each ratio. An empty field — no limit. Percentages as in «Ratios»: dividends 3 = 3%.
3. **The table** updates by itself; «Found: N». A tap on a header sorts (the first tap the useful way: P/E, P/S and beta — smaller first), ☆ in a row picks it, the ticker opens the card. «Show more» — the next 50.
4. **★ Save the screen** — under its name, it joins the ready ones (× deletes).
5. **As a set** — the first 13 results become a set: chart, map of ties, comparison, «what if» risk and basket.

A security without a ratio does not pass a filter on it; TASE shares have P/E and P/S hidden (Yahoo mixes agorot and dollars), so a filter on them leaves only US ones. The quant score of thousands of shares takes about 3 seconds the first time a day, then it is fast.

**Reminder.** A screener gives a list to study, not a list to buy.

**Trap.** Tuning the filters until one «perfect» share is left — and buying it without a thesis, a stop and a check of why it is cheap.

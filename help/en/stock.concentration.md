# Concentration of the profit

**What it is.** What share of the period's profit came from the best 1, 3 and 5 months, and what the total would be without the best 3 months.

**The idea.** If most of the rise came from one spike (a report, rumours of a takeover), it is an event, not a trend, and a repeat cannot be counted on.

**How to use it.** More than half the profit in 1–2 months — the mark «spike». Find that month in the mosaic, look at the news of that day and write in the diary what happened. An even profit is more reliable.

**Reminder.** Without its best month, would it be a good share?

**Trap.** Buying after a spike expecting a second one.

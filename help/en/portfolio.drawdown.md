# Drawdown review

**What it is.** A block on the card of a position 20% or more under its purchase price. It shows not a «fiasco» but the price's state now: how much it takes to get back to the purchase price, how far the price has already moved from its 3-month low, where it stands against its SMA50 and SMA200, the month's move, the quant score and a stop from today's price. From these, a stage: «still falling», «a base is forming», «the rise began», «the trend is up again».

**The idea.** The purchase price matters only to you — the market does not know it. The decision «hold or sell» is made as if buying today. Hence one question: would I buy this share today at this price, knowing what I know?

**How to use it.**

1. Look at the stage. «Still falling» — no low in sight, do not add. «A base is forming» — the fall stopped, but the rise is not confirmed. «The rise began» — the price is above its SMA50; while under its SMA200 it is a rebound. «The trend is up again» — above both averages.
2. Answer «would I buy it today?».
   - **No** → «Would not — sold». Or cut the position to the size you would buy today. The loss already happened, a sale only records it, and the freed money goes to a better idea. A realised loss lowers the tax on the same year's gains (in Israel 25%); check with the broker.
   - **Yes** → «Would buy — hold with a stop». The trade conditions open with a stop from today's price (under the recent low, by ATR). Write the thesis — why it will rise — and place the stop at the broker (the block «Protection»).
3. Do not average down: buy more «to lower the average» only when the price is above its SMA50 and by a new plan with its own stop.
4. The arithmetic: after −20% it takes +25%, after −30% +43%, after −50% +100%. That is why the stop goes in on the day of the purchase (rule 1).

**Reminder.** Would I buy it today at this price? If not — why do I hold it?

**Trap.** Waiting to «at least get back to zero»: a decision by the purchase price, not by the share. While you wait, the money sits in a worse idea.

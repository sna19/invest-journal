# At most per market

**What it is.** How many candidates may come from one market — the US or Israel.

**The idea.** One market can fail as a whole; the limit keeps the candidates from adding up to one bet on a country.

**How to use it.** Fewer — the list is split between markets more evenly. More — allows all the best to be on one exchange. Default 7.

**Reminder.** Am I betting on one country?

**Trap.** Removing the limit because one market's candidates are «better».

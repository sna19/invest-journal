# Sort by

**What it is.** Which number orders the shares that passed the filter. **Mean** — over all years, but one exceptional year can pull it up.

**The idea.** The mean loves outliers, the median the typical year, the worst year stability. Choosing the sort is choosing what you fear.

**How to use it.** **Median** — steadier against one outlier. **Worst year** — shares whose bad year was still bearable rank higher. Default: mean.

**Reminder.** Does the average year matter, or the worst one?

**Trap.** Sorting by the mean when one lucky year made it.

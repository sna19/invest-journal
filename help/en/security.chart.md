# Security card and chart

The security's closing price and its benchmark (S&P 500 for the US, TA-125 for TASE), brought to the same starting point: if the dashed line is above the security's line, the market rose more over the period.

**Marks:**
- ● — a hypothesis entry, ■ — its exit; the colour is the source (labels under the chart);
- ▲ — your purchase, ▼ — a sale (virtual portfolios too, automatic ones not: they repeat the hypotheses).

A tap on a mark opens the hypothesis or the position. Periods — 1 month … 10 years.

**Gestures.** Two fingers — zoom, a finger left-right — scroll, a long press — the price on a date.

Prices are closes on the scale of the last split; trades before a split are recomputed to the same scale.

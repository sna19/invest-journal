# Protection

**What it is.** A ready protective plan: the kind of stop, the levels, the trailing %, the size of the position and the maximum loss — and a cheat sheet to type in at the broker. The stop goes under the nearest support level (the 20- or 60-day low, the 200-day average) with a margin of 0.5 ATR; with no level near — the price minus 3 ATR; never on a round number. The limit is 2% or 1 ATR under the stop, the trailing stop 3 × ATR% within 8–20%. The size of a new position is 1% of the portfolio divided by the loss per share at the stop. The coefficients are in «Settings → Protective plan».

**The idea.** Big losses almost always grow from small ones nobody stopped. A stop is a decision made in advance and calmly, while there is no fear. It goes under a level with a margin for the share's usual move (ATR), so that noise does not hit it; the size of a position comes from how much you are ready to lose. A trailing stop is for what you hold for growth: it follows the price up and keeps the profit on a turn.

**How to use it.** Bought → the cheat sheet at once → placed at the broker → «I placed it». The values in the cheat sheet are copied by a tap. Disagree with a level — change it, but do not leave the position without a stop. A stop is only raised. The app follows a trailing stop's level by the closes itself.

**Reminder.** No stop — the loss has no limit.

**Trap.** Lowering the stop as the price comes near it, or placing it so close that the usual daily noise hits it.

# Picked

**What it is.** The securities you chose. **How they get here:** a quarterly model's candidate opened from the model is picked by that — you opened it, so you chose it; any other security is picked with ☆ on its card. Unpick — ★ again.

**The idea.** Picked securities are your short list of attention: the ones you will come back to on purpose, not by chance.

**How to use it.**

**Pick from a list.** ☆ is not only on a security's card but right in the lists of «Signals» (the week's ideas, «Want to buy», «Review», «Sell»): a tap picks, another unpicks.

**As a set.** On top — «Open as a set»: the picked ones on one chart (the same widget as on the card), on the map of ties and in the comparison table. On the map of ties the portfolios' buttons join their positions to the picked ones — you see which picked share repeats what you already hold; «+ share» adds any security, «×» takes one off the map. The choice is remembered on the device.

**Where they show.** In the model's candidate list the picked ones come first, and those that dropped out when the parameters changed are in a line under it: no need to search for them again. Here — all at once: the price and the day, the **comparison** (the return over a month, three months and a year, valuation, risk, quality — tap a header to sort) and the **map of ties**: close circles move together, a line is a tie above 0.7, a tap on a circle — its most tied neighbours; below — the correlations of all pairs.

**The switch on top** — «Tickers · Model · Picked»: each screen keeps its own (the search and filter, the variant and parameters, the place on the screen), so you can go back and forth. The «Recent» row — the securities opened last: a tap goes straight to the card.

**Reminder.** Why did I pick this security?

**Trap.** Picking everything: the list stops helping you choose.

# Variants A and B

**What it is.** A «Relative» — always the N countries with the best rise. B «Dual» — the same, but a country is taken only if its rise beats the refuge's (BIL — US treasury bills); otherwise its part sits in BIL.

**The idea.** Relative momentum picks the best among countries; absolute momentum (against the bills) answers whether to be in shares at all. Together — «dual momentum»: in long falls B moves into bills.

**How to use it.** Compare the rows A and B in «Result»: B usually has a smaller worst fall and a slightly lower return. A dashed chip in «The signal now» is a part that B moves into BIL.

**Reminder.** Protection costs return in good years.

**Trap.** Judging B by one calm year: its point shows in crashes.

# At most per sector

**What it is.** How many candidates may come from one sector. Fewer — a wider spread, and one industry's failure does not drag the whole list down.

**The idea.** Securities of one sector fall together; the limit makes the list more diverse.

**How to use it.** More — the list is closer to «the best by the numbers» even if all are from one industry. Default 2.

**Reminder.** How many times do I hold the same bet?

**Trap.** Removing the limit and getting five securities of one sector — one bet five times.

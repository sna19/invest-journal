# Country momentum

**What it is.** Once a month — the 3 countries with the best rise over 12 months (leaving out the last one). A country is the exchange fund of its shares (EWJ — Japan, EWZ — Brazil…). In variant B a country is taken only if it beats US treasury bills (BIL), otherwise its part goes into BIL.

**The idea.** Momentum is one of the most persistent market effects, but it «spoils» within months. So the choice must be rebuilt regularly. «Bought last year's leader and hold» is the worst of both worlds; the base «last year's leaders» shows it in numbers.

**How to use it.** «The signal now» — what to hold in the coming month and what to buy or sell against the last one. «Result» — the model against three bases over the whole window and the check years. «Checks» — honesty: parameters chosen without peeking and a comparison with chance. Do the rebalance on the month's first trading day, whole, as the model shows. On those days the model records «Recommendation» hypotheses itself, and the virtual portfolios A and B buy its choice.

**Reminder.** Momentum without rebuilding is chasing the past.

**Trap.** Tuning L and N until the past looks pretty. The best run on history is almost always worse going forward.

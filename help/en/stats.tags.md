# By tags

**What it is.** Tags are your topic labels: «AI», «banks», «dividends». They are set in a hypothesis card, comma-separated, and can change at any time; upper and lower case are the same.

**The idea.** The topics you are strong or weak in show only in numbers; tags gather hypotheses by topic.

**How to use it.**

**When a tag appears.** Once it has 10 closed hypotheses — the same threshold as for sources. Before that the tag is in the «Gathering» line with a count.

**How to read.** The share that came true with its interval and the mean excess; tags are sorted by the lower bound of the interval. One hypothesis can be in several tags.

**Reminder.** In which topics do I guess right more often?

**Trap.** Concluding from a tag with three hypotheses.

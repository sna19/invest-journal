# Did it come true?

**What it is.** At a sale the purchase's entry is shown — the thesis and the expectation — with the question «Did it come true? yes / partly / no / too early». The answer and your conclusion become a conclusion entry in the purchase's chain, with the exit's reason and the rule from the review.

**The idea.** The price after a sale does not say whether the thesis was right. The answer ties the decision to its outcome — so you see what to trust next time.

**How to use it.** Reread the purchase's thesis and answer on substance, not on money: a thesis can come true at a loss and fail at a profit. «Too early» — if you sold before the thesis could show.

**Reminder.** Did what I bought it for come true?

**Trap.** Answering «yes» because you made money.

# What the colours mean

**What it is.** The app's colours, screen by screen. The common rule: **green** — good or a rise, **red** — bad, a fall or needs action, **yellow** — attention, a mixed picture, **grey** — no data, or a decision made without a stop.

**Numbers everywhere**

- A green number with ▲ — a rise, a red one with ▼ — a fall (returns, the day's change, a position's result).
- The small numbers under a percent in the row of periods — the same over the market: green — better than the market, red — worse.

**Quant score** (the dot next to a ticker, the line in a share's card)

- 🟢 green — strong: a total of 65 of 100 or more and the price above its 200-day average.
- 🟡 yellow — mixed: a total of 35–65, or strong but the price under its SMA200.
- 🔴 red — weak: a total under 35.
- ⚪ grey — little data (fewer than three factors). Funds have no dot.
- The factors' letters: A and B — green (the best 40% of the sector), C — the usual colour, D and F — red (the worst 40%).

**Portfolio**

- The dot at the end of a position's row: green — the price is today's live quote, pale grey — the close.
- The protection line on top: a red background — positions without a stop; a green shield — everything is protected.
- Path marks next to a ticker: yellow «fading», «stuck» — the rise stopped; blue «spike», «bounce» — a sharp move, check its reason.

**Protection** (a security's card)

- A green shield «protected» — a stop is placed at the broker.
- A red «not protected» and a red cheat-sheet button — there is no stop.
- A yellow «the price is within 1 ATR of the stop» — the stop may trigger in the next days.
- A grey «no stop: reason» — you decided on no stop deliberately.
- A blue frame around a stop variant — the recommended one.

**Signals**

- Red — action needed: the stop is broken, the model's period is over, bought without a stop.
- Plain — time to review, the target reached, a path mark, a diary reminder.

**Portfolio risk** (Portfolio → Risk)

- Yearly swings: green — up to 15%, yellow — 15–25%, red — over 25%.
- Independent bets: green — at least 60% of the number of securities, yellow — 35–60%, red — under 35% (the securities move together).
- The link to the job: green — up to 0.3, yellow — 0.3–0.6, red — over 0.6.
- A security's label: green «reduces risk», red «duplicates …», no colour — «neutral».
- On the map of links a circle's colour is the security's sector.

**How the profit was made**

- The mosaic: a green cell — a month of rise, red — of fall; the brighter, the stronger (about 10% a month is the full colour); outlined — a month you held the share; italic in the «year» column — an incomplete year.
- The ladder: a green step — the year (quarter) added to the total, red — took from it.

**Models**

- The placebo badge: green «signal» — a random choice finds as many rarely (under 5% of runs), yellow «unclear» — 5–25%, red «chance» — 25% and more.
- A yellow tag on a candidate («short of K», «beyond the sector cap») — it was added by «10 more», not chosen by the model.
- A red line «vs usual quarter» — the share's quarter is hardly better than its usual one.

**Chart**

- A green mark — a purchase, red — a sale, coloured with a label — sources' hypotheses (each source has its colour), small dots on top — days with news (bright — days of a strong move).
- The colours of comparison lines and of the models' curves only tell the lines apart; they mean neither good nor bad.

**The idea.** A colour is for noticing at a glance what to look at. The decision is made by the numbers and the thesis, not by the colour.

**How to use it.** Run your eyes over a screen: red first, yellow next, green needs no action. A tap on a coloured mark or the «?» next to it — what is behind it.

**Reminder.** Red is a reason to look, not an order to sell.

**Trap.** Buying everything green and selling everything red: a colour squeezes several questions into one and does not know your thesis.

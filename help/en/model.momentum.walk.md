# The check without peeking

**What it is.** Every pair of L (3, 6, 9, 12 months) and N (1–5 countries) is run on the years before the check years; the best by Sharpe is then measured on the last 3 years, which took no part in the choice.

**The idea.** Parameters tuned on the whole history always look good. An honest judgement is on years the choice did not see.

**How to use it.** If the result on the check years is close to the «Result» table, the model is not overfitted. If much worse, its past success is largely tuning.

**Reminder.** What counts is what was chosen in advance.

**Trap.** The country funds' history is short, 3 years is little; one lucky year moves the number a lot.

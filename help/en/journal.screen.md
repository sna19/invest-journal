# Diary

**What it is.** All your entries in one feed: notes, observations, decisions and conclusions — newest first, by days. Every place in the app (a security, a portfolio, a sector, a hypothesis, a source, a model run) has its own feed of the same entries.

**The idea.** A thought written at the moment of a decision is more honest than an explanation in hindsight. A month later you see what you thought then and what came after — that is how you learn.

**How to use it.** «+» at the bottom or «Write» in a feed. Search by words (Russian, English, Hebrew), filter by kind, tags (several — all at once), period and hypotheses; the filter stays in the page's address. «Personal» — entries not tied to decisions. What you do not need — «Archive», it can always come back.

**Reminder.** Written before the decision — checked after it.

**Trap.** Writing only about successes: a diary of wins teaches nothing.

# How many countries (N)

**What it is.** How many of the best countries to hold, in equal parts.

**The idea.** One country — a stronger effect, but also the risk of one country. 3–4 is a reasonable middle.

**How to use it.** Watch how «Worst fall» and Sharpe change with N, not only the return.

**Reminder.** Diversity is free protection.

**Trap.** N = 1 because of a pretty history.

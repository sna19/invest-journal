# What if?

**What it is.** A simulator: you pick 1–3 securities and an amount — and see how the independent bets, the portfolio's swings and the tie to your job change, and the label «reduces risk / duplicates».

**The idea.** The same share can be a good buy for one portfolio and a bad one for another. What matters is not only the security itself but what it adds to what you already have.

**How to use it.** Before buying, compare 2–3 candidates with a similar thesis and take the one that diversifies. Try «instead» too: replacing a duplicating position often cuts risk more than a new purchase.

**Reminder.** A new security — a new bet or the same one?

**Trap.** Buying a bad idea only because it «diversifies». First the thesis and the protection plan, then the effect on risk.

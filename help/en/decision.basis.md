# Decision and basis

**What it is.** Every purchase and sale from the form is a decision with its own thesis entry in the diary: buy, add, sell, trim, hold (keep it at a signal), not buy, defer. A decision has one basis: intuition, a source's hypothesis, the model, the consensus or mixed.

**The idea.** Later you see which bases work for you: intuition against the model, the consensus and the sources. Without a basis this cannot be counted.

**How to use it.** In «Bought» the basis comes from the kind of trade — correct it if it is wrong. In «Sold» write «Why»: empty is allowed, but the decision and the position get the mark «no reason». «Reviewed» at a signal is saved as a decision «hold». A decision's key fields do not change; a mistake can be cancelled within 24 hours.

**Reminder.** What is this decision based on — honestly?

**Trap.** Putting «model» when you decided yourself and the model merely did not object.

# Portfolio protection

**What it is.** The share of the portfolio under stops, the maximum loss if every stop is hit, and the list of positions without protection. In «Statistics» — sales of positions with a stop against those without, and the share of days the portfolio was fully protected.

**The idea.** While even one position has no stop, the portfolio's maximum loss is unknown. A known risk can be carried calmly; an unknown one cannot.

**How to use it.** The goal is 100% protected. A red line → «Protect all» → go through the list top down. The maximum loss is more than you can bear — make the positions smaller, do not remove the stops.

**Reminder.** Do I know how much I can lose right now?

**Trap.** «It is long-term, it needs no stop» — a position without a stop is a decision made explicitly and recorded with its reason, not by default.

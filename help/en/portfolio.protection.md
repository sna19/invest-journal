# Portfolio protection

**What it is.** The share of the portfolio under stops, the maximum loss if every stop is hit, and the list of positions without protection. In «Statistics» — sales of positions with a stop against those without, and the share of days the portfolio was fully protected.

**The idea.** While even one position has no stop, the portfolio's maximum loss is unknown. A known risk can be carried calmly; an unknown one cannot.

**How to use it.** **Prices and percents at once.** Each level shows everything together: the price, how many percent it is under today's price (or a target — over it), what it is on one share and on the whole position, what part of the portfolio, and what the stop keeps or loses against the purchase price. In the cheat sheet each line is a pair «price ↔ %»: type a price — the percent is computed, type a percent — the price is (for a trailing stop — «%» and «stop now», for the limit — the percent under the stop). At the bottom — the summary as professionals keep it: the **risk** (what the stop loses when it fires, in money and in % of the portfolio), the **target** (what you would make) and the **ratio 1 : N** — what can be made per unit of risk; under 1 : 1.5 a trade is usually not worth the risk.

 The goal is 100% protected. A red line → «Protect all» → go through the list top down. The maximum loss is more than you can bear — make the positions smaller, do not remove the stops.

**Reminder.** Do I know how much I can lose right now?

**Trap.** «It is long-term, it needs no stop» — a position without a stop is a decision made explicitly and recorded with its reason, not by default.

# Rebalance

**What it is.** How often the choice is revisited: on the first trading day of every month or quarter (January, April, July, October).

**The idea.** Momentum ages within months. Less often — fewer trades and taxes, but a bigger delay.

**How to use it.** Compare both modes: the costs are shown under the «Result» table.

**Reminder.** Rebalance by the calendar, not by mood.

**Trap.** Skipping a rebalance because «it is rising anyway».

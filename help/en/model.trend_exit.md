# Trend exit

**What it is.** An early sale inside the quarter if a monthly close is below the 3-month average.

**The idea.** A trend stop cuts big falls, but in a sideways market it knocks you out of positions that rise afterwards. You pay part of the return for less risk.

**How to use it.** In the comparison table decide what matters more: the excess or the worst quarter. Once chosen — follow it without exceptions.

**Reminder.** The exit is decided before the entry.

**Trap.** Cancelling an exit that fired «just this once».

# Rolling return

**What it is.** A line where each point is the rise over the previous 12 months (a window of 6, 12 or 36 — a setting); the second line is the same over the market.

**The idea.** A period's total is one picture, the rolling line is a film. It shows when the rise began to fade, long before it shows in the 3-year total.

**How to use it.** The line goes to zero — the momentum weakens, even if the price is still near its high. The «vs market» line under zero — the share lags the market over the last 12 months.

**Reminder.** Where is the line going now — up or to zero?

**Trap.** Selling at the first bend down: the line swings; look at how many months in a row it falls.

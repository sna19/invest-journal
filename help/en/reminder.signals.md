# Signals

**What it is.** A block on top of the main screen: the diary's reminders that fired and the trades' signals — stop broken, target reached, time to review, the model's period over.

**The idea.** Everything that needs a decision today is in one place. What was snoozed comes back by itself, and the count shows when you snooze too often.

**How to use it.** A trade's signal — tap it, and in the card «Sold» or «Reviewed». A reminder — «Close with a conclusion» (the conclusion goes into the entry's chain) or «Snooze»: a day, a week, a month or until a date. From the third time the count turns red.

**Reminder.** What do I decide on this signal today?

**Trap.** Snoozing again and again is a decision too, just an unconscious one.

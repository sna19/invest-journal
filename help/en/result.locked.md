# Why fields cannot change

**What it is.** After saving you cannot change the security, signal type, direction, thesis, horizon, confidence, the «I was wrong» condition, mode or entry price. This guards against fooling yourself: once the result is visible, it is very tempting to «adjust» the horizon or the direction — and the source's stats stop meaning anything.

**The idea.** An unchangeable record is what makes the journal honest: a past decision cannot be «corrected» to fit the result.

**How to use it.**

**What you can change.** Notes and tags — at any time. After closing — the conclusion and the reason for closing early.

**An input mistake.** Within 24 hours a record can be cancelled with the button in the card — and recorded again. Later it cannot be cancelled: the hypothesis stays in the stats.

**It cannot be deleted.** Neither through the interface nor otherwise: the ban is in the database itself.

**Reminder.** What was written in advance is not rewritten.

**Trap.** Cancelling and recording again to change the confidence or the term.

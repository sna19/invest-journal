# Relative strength

**What it is.** The share's price divided by its market's index, brought to 100 at the start of the period.

**The idea.** The line rises — the share is stronger than the market; flat — it just rides with the market; falls — weaker. Funds' momentum strategies are built on this.

**How to use it.** Look at the slope over the last 6–12 months. The price rises but the line is flat or falls — the market made the rise, not the share.

**Reminder.** Is the share strong or is the market rising?

**Trap.** Comparing with someone else's market: an Israeli share is compared with the TA-125, an American one with the S&P 500.

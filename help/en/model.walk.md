# Check without peeking

To judge the model on the past honestly, candidates are chosen only on the 7 years **before** the tested year and measured in it. The tested year took no part in the choice. This is done for each of the last three years.

**How to read.** «They» — the mean result of the chosen in the tested year, «All» — the mean of the whole universe in the same year. If the chosen are steadily better than all, the selection finds something.

**Careful.** The base lacks shares that vanished in those years (bankruptcies, takeovers), so the past looks better than it was. The check is for reference only. Only hypotheses recorded ahead count in the model's rating.

# A source's automatic portfolio

A virtual portfolio the app keeps by itself: each hypothesis of the source of the types «Recommendation», «Target price» and «Opinion» on a rise is bought for a fixed amount (by default ₪10,000) at the hypothesis entry price and sold when it closes — on time or early. Dividends during the holding come as cash. Hypotheses on a fall and news do not enter the portfolio.

**Why.** To compare sources in money — next to your real portfolio: what would have happened had you bought everything it advises.

**Turning on.** In the source card. A portfolio turned off goes to the archive with its history; turned on again — it continues.

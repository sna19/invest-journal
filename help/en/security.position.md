# My position

**What it is.** A block at the top of the security's card: the portfolio, quantity, value, the change today and since the purchase, how long it is held, the trade's conditions and the position's whole history — with the pencil, cancel, reversal, «Bought», «Sold» and delete. If the security is in several portfolios — a block for each, the one opened from on top.

**The idea.** A decision on a position is made next to the security's chart, news and data, not in a separate list. One card — from the portfolio, the model, a hypothesis and a notification.

**How to use it.** A tap on a position in the portfolio opens the security's card. Look at «Trade conditions» and the signals first, then the chart and «Why it moves». Closed positions are under «Closed positions» at the bottom of the card.

**Reminder.** Would I buy it today at this price?

**Trap.** Looking only at «since the purchase» and holding the position to get back to the entry price.

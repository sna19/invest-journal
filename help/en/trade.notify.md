# Notifications

**What it is.** The trade discipline's signals come to the phone: stop broken, target reached, time to review, the model's period is over. A tap opens the position with «Sold» and «Reviewed».

**The idea.** A stop you do not hear about in time does not protect. The banner in the app is seen only when you open it; a notification comes by itself — while there is still time to decide.

**How to use it.** On an iPhone add the app to the home screen (Safari → «Share» → «Add to Home Screen»), open it from there and tap «Turn on for this device». «Test» sends a trial notification. Untick the signals you do not need. Each signal comes once; after «Reviewed» — again if the condition is met.

**Reminder.** A stop came — check the execution at the broker and enter the sale.

**Trap.** On a signal, «reviewing» the stop lower to make the notification go away.

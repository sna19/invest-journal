# Weekly pick

**What it is.** The entries of the last three months whose security moved more than 10% since they were written, and the reminders snoozed three times or more.

**The idea.** A big move after an entry is a reason to reread what you thought then: were you right, is the thesis broken. A reminder snoozed three times is a decision you avoid.

**How to use it.** Once a week go through the list: open the security, reread the entry, answer with an observation or a conclusion. Snoozed three times — decide or close it.

**Reminder.** What has changed since I wrote it?

**Trap.** Rereading only the entries about securities that rose.

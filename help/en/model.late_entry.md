# Late entry and laggards

**What it is.** Candidates that did not rise in the first 2 months of the quarter, and a check over 10 years: do such ones catch up in the 3rd month.

**The idea.** «It is about time it rose» is the gambler's fallacy: the market does not have to catch up. The price may have stood still because it took in bad news. A rule «buy the laggards» is allowed only if past candidates show it better than chance.

**How to use it.** While the verdict is «unclear» or «do not catch up» — simulation only. Real money — only with «catch up» and the parameter on.

**Reminder.** Lagging is not a debt the market owes you. Has it been checked that laggards catch up?

**Trap.** Confusing «did not rise» with «undervalued».

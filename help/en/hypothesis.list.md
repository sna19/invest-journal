# Hypothesis list

Everything you recorded from articles: open hypotheses on top, the nearest horizon first.

**A row.** Ticker and direction (▲ up, ▼ down), the security's name, the source and the signal type, days to the horizon. «Default» — the article named no horizon, 3 months are set.

**«Awaits a price».** The entry price is the close of the first trading day since the record date. The daily price job sets it; until then no result is computed. With prices the current result against the benchmark appears here: `▲ +4.2%`.

**Filters.** Open, closed or all; mode (simulation or real money); market; «Default term» — only hypotheses whose horizon is not from the article. The choice is remembered.

**Gestures.** Swipe a row right — a note; left — closing early with a reason. Pull the list down — refresh. Tap a row — the card with all fields.

A hypothesis cannot be deleted — only an input error can be cancelled within 24 hours (in the card).

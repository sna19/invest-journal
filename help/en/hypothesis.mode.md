# Mode

**What it is.** **Simulation** — the default. You check the source's claim without investing money.

**The idea.** Separating simulation from money shows whether you pick the worst of your ideas for real trades.

**How to use it.**

**Real money** — you really bought or sold the security on this idea. You can give the amount.

**How it counts.** The result is computed the same way: by the excess over the benchmark. But in the stats the modes are separate rows: that shows whether the ideas you acted on differ from the rest, and whether you pick the worst of what you read for your money.

The mode does not change after saving.

**Reminder.** Is this idea for money or for testing?

**Trap.** Marking only the lucky trades as «real money» after the fact.

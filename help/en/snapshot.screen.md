# Snapshots

**What it is.** A diary entry can keep a snapshot of what you saw: a security's chart, the comparison with sectors and indices, the model's candidates, a portfolio's composition, a «What if?» result, a photo or a screenshot. The numbers themselves are kept — the snapshot is drawn by the same charts and tables, it can be touched, but it is never updated.

**The idea.** A month later what matters is what you looked at when you decided, not today's picture. A snapshot is an honest memory of the screen.

**How to use it.** On the chart, in «Compare with», in the candidates' list, in the portfolio and in «What if?» — the button «To the diary»: an entry opens with the snapshot. In any entry — «Photo or screenshot»: a picture from the iPhone is made about 1600 px and kept on the NAS, in the backup. When the model records its quarter's list, an entry with the candidates' snapshot and a reminder «quarter review» appear by themselves.

**Reminder.** What did I see when I decided?

**Trap.** Explaining a past decision with today's chart.

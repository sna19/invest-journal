# Default horizon

If the article names no horizon, 3 months are set — always the same.

**Why not choose by mood.** If you set the horizon with hindsight — longer for a «long» idea, shorter for a «quick» one — you unwittingly fit the check to your expectation, and the source's stats lie. One rule makes all such hypotheses comparable.

**Why 3 months.** Long enough for news or a recommendation to show in the price, and quick enough for the source to gather closed hypotheses for a rating.

Hypotheses with the default horizon are marked «default» and picked by the «Default term» filter: that shows whether their results differ from those with a horizon from the article.

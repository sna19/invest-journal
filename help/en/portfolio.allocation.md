# Allocation

What the portfolio consists of, by value. The measure is chosen with the buttons above the chart:

- **Securities** — the eight largest, the rest together.
- **Sectors** — technology, financials, healthcare and others. A fund (ETF) is split across sectors by what it holds: if SCHD has 16% technology, 16% of its value goes to «Technology». What a fund holds outside shares is shown as «Other in funds».
- **Industries** and **countries** — for single stocks; funds are gathered into the «Funds (ETF)» row.
- **Company size** — by market capitalisation in dollars: mega (over 200B), large (10–200B), mid (2–10B), small (under 2B).
- **Stocks and funds**, **markets** (US, Israel), **currencies** (dollar, shekel; the portfolio's cash in its own currency).
- **Result** — how much of the value is in positions in profit and how much at a loss.

**Bars or pie** — the switch at the top right. On the pie, tap a slice or a legend row and its share appears in the centre. The measure and the view are remembered on this device.

**Where the data come from.** Sector, industry, country and market cap come from the security's Yahoo profile. It loads by itself when a security enters a portfolio and refreshes monthly. Until it loads, the security stands in the «No data» row.

Grey rows — cash, «Others», «No data» — are not categories of their own.

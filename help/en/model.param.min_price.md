# Price at least

**What it is.** The minimum share price at the end of the last month in dollars (for TASE — the shekel equivalent).

**The idea.** Very cheap shares jerk around and trade poorly: their «seasonality» is more often noise.

**How to use it.** Higher — fewer penny shares with ragged charts and data errors. Lower — a wider universe. Default $5.

**Reminder.** Can the security be bought and sold without losses?

**Trap.** Removing the price threshold for a longer list.

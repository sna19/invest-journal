# Commodities in the quarterly model

**What it is.** The same quarterly model, but on exchange funds of commodities instead of shares: precious and industrial metals, energy, agriculture and broad indices. Variants A «Price rise» and B «Against the market» (the market is the broad commodity fund DBC), with their own parameters, hypotheses and virtual portfolios «Quarterly — commodities, A / B». The list of funds is edited on the model screen; leveraged and short funds are shown but left out.

**The idea.** A commodity's season often has an economic reason: the heating season, the driving season, the harvest. So it is worth checking apart from the shares. What is checked is the fund you can buy: futures funds (USO, UNG and others) lose part of the return when rolling to the next contract, and their season may differ from the commodity's own.

**How to use it.** «Universe → Commodities». Look at how many funds passed and how many would pass by chance (the placebo), and at the check without peeking. There are few funds (about twenty), so 3 candidates by default and at most 2 from one group; funds of the same thing (GLD and IAU — both gold) are not taken together. Many funds have less than 15 years of history: «Years of history» can go down to 8.

**Reminder.** A commodity's season is a reason in the economy, not just numbers of past years.

**Trap.** Buying a leveraged fund for a season: its price melts over time whatever the season.

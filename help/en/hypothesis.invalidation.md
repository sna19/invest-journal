# «I was wrong if…»

The condition under which you admit the hypothesis failed — written down before the result is known.

**Good conditions** are checkable and specific:

- «Next quarter's revenue below the company's forecast»
- «The stock falls below 120 before the horizon»
- «The regulator does not approve the deal by March»
- «The margin falls for the second quarter in a row»

**Bad ones** are vague: «if something goes wrong», «if the market falls».

The condition does not close the hypothesis by itself: the result is the excess over the benchmark at the horizon. It is for you — so as not to change your mind in hindsight, and to have something to compare with in the conclusion after closing.

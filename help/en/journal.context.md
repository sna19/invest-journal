# Then → now

**What it is.** When an entry is saved, the numbers of the moment are copied into it for each linked security and portfolio: the price, the change for the day and the month, against the market for the month, the weight in the portfolio. They never change; next to them — the numbers now.

**The idea.** Memory adjusts the past to the result. Frozen numbers show at what price and in what setting you thought so.

**How to use it.** Open «then → now» at an old entry: how far the price went and whether you were right. For old notes moved into the diary the numbers are restored from the prices of that date — this is marked.

**Reminder.** What was known then?

**Trap.** Judging an old thought by today's price without looking at what it was at the moment of writing.
